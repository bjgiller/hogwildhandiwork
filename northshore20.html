5.Check-In & Players Meeting

Check-in is at the Pavilion near hole 17's tee pad. It opens at 7:45 AM and closes at 8:30 AM. Every player must check in — this is mandatory.

There is no in-person players meeting anywhere this year. This page is the players meeting. Please read the whole thing before you arrive.

6.Scoring

Keeping score on PDGA Live is required. On each card, at least one player must run live scoring with the event code Northshore20.

Run live scoring here: pdga.com/apps/tournament/score — enter code Northshore20 to load the event.

We also have paper scorecards available at check-in as a backup — but a live PDGA Live score must still be submitted for every group.

7.OB & Course Notes

Full scores and OB are tracked on PDGA Live and summarized below. If there is ever a discrepancy, PDGA Live is the official record.

Round 2 basket moves: the baskets on holes 3, 5, 9, 11, 12, and 13 will be moved during lunch for Round 2. Round 1 plays the current layout positions.

  • Hole 3258 ftNo OB.
  • Hole 4353 ftNo OB.
  • Hole 5225 ftOB surrounded by water, indicated by the white line near the basket.
  • Hole 6292 ftOB on or across the sidewalk.

  • Hole 8307 ftOB long, defined by the white line protecting 9's fairway (45 ft long of the basket).

  • Hole 11374 ftOB surrounded by water.
  • Hole 12297 ftOB surrounded by water.
  • Hole 13220 ftTriple MandoDiscs must fly below the rope and between the two wooden posts. If the mando is missed, re-tee with a 1-stroke penalty. OB surrounded by water; long defined by the white line near the basket.
  • Hole 14351 ftOB on or across the sidewalk.
  • Hole 15203 ftTriple MandoDiscs must go between the wooden fence and below the bottom-most rope of the banner. If the mando is missed, proceed to the drop zone with a 1-stroke penalty. OB on or across the sidewalk. You don't have to throw the gap on the drive — you may lay up.
  • Hole 16361 ftNo OB.
  • Hole 17380 ftOB if surrounded by water.
