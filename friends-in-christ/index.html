Who We Are

A church family stepping into faith, together

We're a congregation of Christ-followers who gather to worship Jesus, grow in personal faith, and reach out with His love to everyone we meet — neighbors, newcomers, and old friends alike.

"Proclaim the crucified and risen Christ. Reach out to everyone with God's love. Grow in faith. Build new and deeper friendships."
  • Lutheran Church—Missouri Synod (LCMS) congregation
  • Casual, come-as-you-are atmosphere — no dress code, all are welcome
  • Sunday School for all ages at 9:30 AM, Worship at 10:30 AM
  • A second service for our Fox Ridge community at 2:30 PM Sundays

Meet Our Pastor

Rev. Emil L. Woerner

Pastor, Friends in Christ Lutheran Church · Serving since July 2009

Pastor Woerner holds a Master of Divinity from Concordia Theological Seminary in Fort Wayne, Indiana, and a Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Tennessee. Before entering the ministry, he spent 18 years as an engineer with International Paper Company. He and his family enjoy hunting, fishing, sports, and marathon running. Pastor Woerner has two children, Emil Jr. and Addie.

The Kids Closet

A not-for-profit charitable project started in 2008 with the Bryant Rotary Club, supplying clothing and basic necessities to children in crisis situations.

Bible Study

Sunday morning Bible study groups for all ages, digging into Scripture together as a family of faith.

🎵

Music Ministry

Our worship is led by keyboard, guitar, and vocalists from within the congregation, offering praise each week.

🤝

Rotary Partnership
